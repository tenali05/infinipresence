infiniPresence

Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

infiniPresence is a workplace attendance app. Employees use it to check in and out at their office with a face scan and their phone's location. This service is run by {{ org }} ("we"), the employer that gave you access. This policy covers the infiniPresence Android app, the iPhone app and the /presence web page.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Face data: one reference photo of your face, taken when you register, and a face measurement (a list of 128 numbers) calculated from it. Each check-in also takes a photo and a new measurement.
  • Precise location: your phone's GPS position at the moment you check in or out, and while the check-in screen is open (to show how far you are from your office). The app does not use your location in the background.
  • Attendance records: the time of each check-in and check-out, its location, and which office you are assigned to.

How we use it

  • To confirm that the person checking in is you, by comparing the new face measurement with your registered one.
  • To confirm that you are within your office's check-in radius.
  • To keep attendance records for your employer (who was present, when, and for how long).
  • Check-in photos are kept so an admin can review a check-in if there is a dispute.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your employer, except the hosting provider that runs the server on our behalf, or where the law requires it. Face data is never used for any purpose other than attendance check-in.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored on your employer's infiniPresence server. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Face photos are private files that only you and your employer's infiniPresence admins can open. The face scan itself runs on your phone; the phone sends the measurement and photo to the server.

Camera and location permissions

The app asks for the camera to take your face photo, and for precise location to check you are at your office. Both are used only while you are using the app. If you deny either permission you can't check in, but you can still sign in and see your status.

How long we keep it

  • Your registered face photo and measurement: until you delete your account, an admin resets your face, or your access ends.
  • Check-in photos: until you delete your account, or your employer deletes them.
  • Check-in times and locations: as long as your employer needs them for its attendance and payroll records.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: sign in, then tap Delete my account at the bottom of the screen.
  • On the web: sign in at /presence and choose Delete my account.

Children

infiniPresence is for employees and is not meant for anyone under 16.
